# Ascend OS Product Notes

## Vision

Ascend OS will become a central operating system for agency revenue, lead generation, sales execution, client fulfillment, attribution, personal-brand planning, and coordinated AI agents.

## Initial Users

- Founders need operating visibility, team administration, audit trails, and future revenue command surfaces.
- Salespeople need focused access to leads, calls, pipeline, and personal performance without sensitive company administration.

## Long-Term Modules

- Revenue Command Center
- Lead Engine
- Sales Workspace and Dialer
- CRM and Stripe
- Client and Fulfillment System
- Financial Command Center
- Advertising Attribution
- Personal Brand System
- Agentic Automation
- White-Label SaaS

## Milestone 1 Scope

This milestone creates the application foundation: secure account access, organization tenancy, Founder and Salesperson roles, granular permissions, onboarding, protected dashboard shells, PWA installability, audit logging, automated tests, and documentation.

## Deferred Features

Real lead generation, dialers, SMS, Stripe, banking, forecasting, client management, contracts, advertising APIs, social publishing, autonomous AI agents, OpenAI or Claude usage, Mac mini workers, workflow builders, and white-label billing are intentionally deferred.

## Core Daily Operating Loop

Future milestones will help users review the command center, act on priority work, move leads and clients through workflows, approve agent recommendations, and monitor revenue and fulfillment health.
